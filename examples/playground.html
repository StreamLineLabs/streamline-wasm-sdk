🎓 Getting Started Tutorial

1 Connect to Streamline

Click Connect above to establish a WebSocket connection to your Streamline server.

2 Send Your First Message

Enter a JSON message in the Produce panel and click Send Message. Try: {"user":"alice","action":"click"}

3 Subscribe to Messages

Click Subscribe in the Subscribe panel to see messages in real-time. Then send more messages!

4 Try Batch Production

Click Send 100 to produce 100 random messages and watch them stream in the subscriber.
